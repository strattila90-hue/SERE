# B4 | Evasion in the field, signalling, link-up | BRAVO · based on FM 21-76-1 and ATP 3-50.21

## B4.1 | Planning movement in hostile territory | web
- **Cycle:** observe → move → observe. Short bounds, frequent halts, and always know where you'll hide if you must.
- **Time of day:** move before dawn, at dusk and at night; rest in a hide by day.
- **Route:** dense vegetation, low ground, shaded slopes. Avoid roads, trails, ridgelines, open ground and settlements.
- **Crossing a road:** at a bend or where the view is shortest; observe for a long time first, then cross at right angles, quickly, one at a time.
- **Crossing a stream:** at the widest, shallowest point, with footwear on, angled against the current; keep spare clothes dry.
! Impatience and fatigue are the main reasons evaders get caught. Better slow but unseen.

## B4.2 | Tracks and how to hide them | web
Anything you leave can be found: footprints, broken twigs, flattened grass, dew brushed off, overturned stones, litter, smell, faeces.
- Step on hard ground, rock, roots and leaf litter; avoid soft, muddy ground.
- Don't break branches; bend them and let them spring back.
- Bury rubbish and faeces, and leave your hide as you found it.
- Walking in water is only worth it for short stretches – it chills you, and the exit point gives you away.
- Don't come back the same way; vary your direction but keep your general heading.

## B4.3 | Defeating observation | web
- **Drones, aerial reconnaissance:** if you hear one, **freeze** – movement is what stands out most. Stay under the canopy, in shade, among irregular shapes.
- **Thermal imaging:** body heat glows against a cold background. Hide behind soil, rock or dense vegetation; open ground offers no good hiding place.
- **Night vision:** the smallest light is visible from far away: phone, watch, cigarette.
- **Search dogs:** a dog follows scent, not sight. Time and distance are your best protection – keep moving, don't panic, don't try to fight.
- **Electronics:** a switched-on phone reveals your position. Turn it on only in the planned time window.

## B4.4 | Improvised signalling and link-up | web
- **Signal mirror:** make a V with two fingers, frame the target (aircraft) in the V, and walk the light spot between your fingers. In sunshine it can be seen tens of kilometres away.
- **Ground signals** (at least 3 m long, high contrast): **V** need assistance, **X** need medical help, **N** no, **Y** yes, **→** travelling this way.
- **Radio / phone:** only in the time window of the communications plan, with a short message (who, where, condition).
- **Linking up with rescuers:** stay hidden until you've identified them; weapon on safe, hands visible, don't run toward them; follow instructions exactly, answer the authentication questions (ISOPREP).
+ Signalling cuts both ways: rescuers and the enemy both see it. Assess the threat first.
